# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (alpha, 2026-11-22)

- Stroke contrast: horizontal strokes are about 8% thinner than vertical ones, and the stroke ends are very slightly
  flattened, which reads as more refined than a uniform line.
- Taller x-height (520 to 545 units) for easier reading at 13 to 14px.
- Redrawn curly quotes (round dot with a tail), ampersand and @.
- Fixed a bug where overlapping strokes could cancel each other (the SemiBold arrow lost its shaft); each stroke is now
  outlined separately and merged.

## 0.1.0 (alpha, 2026-11-22)

- Plume Regular, Medium, SemiBold, drawn from strokes (no other font as a source). Basic Latin, Latin-1 accents, Romanian,
  symbols, currency, arrows. Tabular figures. A first kerning table.
